Privacy policy

rclone-dissertation · effective 6 October 2026

Scope

This policy covers the OAuth client "rclone-dissertation", used with the open-source tool rclone by its owner, Sangwook Joo, to back up the owner's dissertation materials to the owner's Google Drive. The app has no users other than the owner.

Google data the app accesses

The app requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. With it, rclone lists, uploads and updates files in the owner's own Google Drive folders that hold the backup. It does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

How the data is used

  • Files are copied from the owner's research computer to the owner's Google Drive, and folder listings are read to decide which files need copying.
  • No data is collected, stored or processed by anyone other than the owner. There is no server, database, analytics or advertising behind this app.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

The OAuth access token is stored only in the rclone configuration file on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner's account. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account page under "Third-party apps with account access".

Compliance

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact
